## 1. Where to send a notice

Send notices to **{{TAKEDOWN_EMAIL}}** or by post to {{COMPANY_ADDRESS}}. Our Turkish-language and English notice forms are at {{WEBSITE_URL}}/legal/takedown. Notices to our registered electronic mail address ({{KEP_ADDRESS}}) are also accepted.

Please identify the hosted address (URL or domain) so we can locate the content.

## 2. Contents of a notice

A notice should include:

1. your full name, company, address, email and telephone number;
2. the exact URL(s) or other location(s) of the content;
3. a clear description of the content and why it is unlawful or infringes your rights;
4. for copyright and trademark claims: identification of the work or mark and proof of ownership or authority to act;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the rights holder, its agent or the law;
6. a statement, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorised to act;
7. your signature (electronic signature is acceptable).

Notices that omit these elements may be returned for completion or may not be acted upon.

## 3. Our process

1. **Acknowledgement.** We acknowledge receipt within {{ACK_DAYS}} business days.
2. **Assessment.** We check the notice is complete and identify the customer account responsible.
3. **Action.** Where the content is manifestly unlawful, or the notice is complete and infringement is apparent, we may disable access or suspend the affected resources. For content where legal judgement is needed, we may ask the customer to respond or wait for a court or authority order.
4. **Notice to customer.** We inform the customer of the removal or restriction and the reasons, except where the law or an authority order prohibits this.
5. **Decision.** We tell the notifier of the outcome, and of any right to counter-notice.

Authorities and courts may order removal directly. Access bans under Law No. 5651 Art. 8 and Art. 9 are executed within the time limits set by law. [Lawyer to confirm the current deadlines.]

## 4. Counter-notice

A customer whose content was removed may send a counter-notice to {{TAKEDOWN_EMAIL}} containing:

1. their name, address and contact details;
2. identification of the removed material and its previous location;
3. a statement, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that they believe in good faith the material was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. consent to the jurisdiction of the courts where the customer resides, or of Turkish courts for non-consumers;
5. their signature.

Where we receive a valid counter-notice under the DMCA-style process, we may restore the material after {{COUNTER_NOTICE_DAYS}} business days unless the rights holder has filed a court action. Turkish-law challenges to access bans follow the procedure in Law No. 5651 and must be brought before the competent authority or court.
