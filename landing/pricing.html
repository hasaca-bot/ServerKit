Pricing questions

How does yearly billing work?

Yearly billing charges ten months for twelve, so you get two months free on workspace plans and AI-managed VPS.

Do compute and database prices add to the workspace plan?

Yes. The workspace plan sets your limits and features. Each service and database is priced by its size on top.

What does the free tier do when idle?

Free compute sleeps after 15 minutes without traffic. Paid instances stay on.

Are MCP calls billed separately?

No. The MCP endpoint is included. Plans limit how many MCP keys you can create.

Does the price include VAT?

No. Prices are in USD and exclude VAT.
